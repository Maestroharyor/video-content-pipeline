# Best practices: official specs, then heuristics

Last checked 2026-11-04. Re-check the official pages at the start of each thumbnail (Step 1) and update this file when
they change.

## Official (YouTube Help)
- **[Add video thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)**:
  - 16:9 for videos; recommended **3840x2160**, minimum width 640 px, "as large as possible".
  - JPG or PNG; **2 MB from mobile, 50 MB from desktop**.
  - Shorts: 9:16, recommended 2160x3840, custom thumbnails only from desktop Studio.
  - A vertical video's 16:9 custom thumbnail is replaced by an auto-generated 4:5 one on home, explore and subscriptions.
  - An account must be verified to upload custom thumbnails; there's a daily upload limit; Community Guidelines apply (a strike can remove thumbnail privileges).
  - Pipeline (house rule, 2026-11-04): always one PNG under 2 MB, which every upload path accepts; the largest PNG that fits
    (usually 1920-2560 wide) is well above the 1280 most screens show.
- **[Test & compare thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)**:
  - Up to **3 thumbnails** per video, tested after publishing.
  - The winner is chosen by **watch time share**, not CTR: a clickbait thumbnail that loses viewers early loses the test.
  - Not available for made-for-kids, mature-audience or private videos.
  - Pipeline: offer a trio (the pick plus two real alternates).

## Heuristics (widely repeated, not primary research; treat percentages in blogs as unsourced)
- Readable at phone size: most impressions are small. 2-4 words, bold sans (KoHo here), high contrast.
- Keep text in the upper part and away from the bottom-right badge (the probe enforces our zones).
- An expressive face often helps; faceless works when the picture is proof (a real logo, a real screen).
- One focal subject; contrast the ground against the niche's dominant colours (the shelf test shows this).
- Thumbnail and title as a pair: the thumbnail adds to the title, never repeats it.
- Optimise for watch time, not just clicks: the thumbnail must be honest to the video (Test & compare rewards that).
